GigFee

Tax invoicing for Australian musicians.

GigFee turns a gig into a tax-compliant invoice in under a minute — the correct Tax Invoice heading, ABN validation, GST worked out either way, and superannuation for performers shown properly rather than guessed at.

  • Professional PDF invoices, emailed or shared from your phone
  • A gig diary that tells you what you are owed and what you have not invoiced yet
  • Recurring invoices for residencies, and fee splits with your band
  • Due-date reminders, reports, and spreadsheet export
Your data stays yours. GigFee works on your phone with no sign-up and no account, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. Cloud backup is optional, stored in Australia, and readable only by the account you sign in with.
