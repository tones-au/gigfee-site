Super, tax and invoicing for Australian musicians

Nobody teaches this part at music school. These are plain answers to the paperwork side of playing for a living in Australia, written for what the rules actually are in 2026 rather than what they were when your last accountant explained them.

Everything here is general information rather than advice, and every page says where its facts came from so you can check them. If something changes, the pages change with it.
