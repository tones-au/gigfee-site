Made for the way gigs actually work

Every part of GigFee came from a real piece of paperwork that musicians end up doing at midnight, on a phone, in a car park.

Invoices that hold up

An invoice a venue's bookkeeper can pay without sending it back. GigFee gets the details right so you do not have to keep them in your head.

  • Correct Tax Invoice heading, with your ABN checked as you type
  • GST handled whether your prices include it or not
  • Superannuation for performers shown properly rather than guessed at
  • A professional PDF, emailed or shared straight from your phone

Split a fee without airing it

Divide a night's money between the band equally, by percentage, or by set amounts. The client is still billed once for the full fee, and none of the split appears on the invoice they receive.

GigFee keeps track of who you still owe, so money that only passed through your account on its way to someone else does not end up counted as your income.

Know what you are owed

A gig diary that carries through to the money. Bring bookings in from your phone's calendar, see what is coming up, and find the gigs you played but never invoiced.

  • Projected earnings for the month or the financial year
  • Due date reminders, so chasing is not left to memory
  • Recurring invoices for a residency
  • Reports and spreadsheet export your accountant can work from

Your books stay yours

Your invoices carry your clients' details and everything you earn. That is not something to hand over lightly, so GigFee is built not to ask for it.

No account required

Install it and start invoicing. There is no sign-up, and you can use GigFee for years without ever telling us who you are.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising and no third party trackers. Nothing in here watches how you use it.

Backup is your choice

Cloud backup stays off until you turn it on. When you do, the copy is stored in Australia and readable only by the account you signed in with.

The full detail is in the privacy policy, including how to delete everything if you decide you are done.
